Submission Guidelines.

We only accept tracks you wrote and produced yourself.

Share a private, unlisted link — no download-required files.

High Quality Mix/Master

Send your best possible mix — we evaluate final quality closely.

Unreleased Preferred

Unreleased tracks give us the most flexibility to plan a release.

Professional Metadata

Correct title, artist name and genre tagging speeds up review.
