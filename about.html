Our Story

Founded on One Idea.

SEOX Records was founded two producers known as H3LL HUNT3R & BRYX in the funk industry who were frustrated with how independent artists were treated by the traditional label system — slow, opaque, and built around control rather than partnership. We set out to build the opposite: a label where artists keep visibility into their own careers.

What started as a single distribution deal has grown into a full-service independent label spanning electronic, bass and global rhythm genres, working with artists across more than 179 countries.

Mission

FAQ

Common Questions.

Do I keep the rights to my music?
Yes. SEOX distributes and promotes your release — you retain ownership of your masters and publishing.
How long does the review process take?
Our A&R team typically responds within 2–3 working days of submission, whatever the outcome.
What genres does SEOX sign?
Primarily electronic, bass and global rhythm genres — from melodic techno to Brazilian funk — but we listen to everything.
Is there a cost to submit?
No. Submission and review are completely free for artists.
Can I see my royalties in real time?
Yes — signed artists get access to a private dashboard with up-to-date streaming and royalty data.
